## Work done today

- Closed the gap where the **CRM-platform subscription** — what a tenant pays to use the CRM
  itself — had **zero Super Admin visibility or control**. Super Admin could manage AI credits
  but could not see or touch platform subscriptions at all.

- Built a **cross-tenant overview**: every tenant in one list with its organization, owner,
  active/disabled status, current platform plan, AI access state, credit balance and lifetime
  revenue — searchable by organization, slug, owner email or tenant id, and filterable by date range.

- Built a **per-tenant detail view**: full user roster, complete platform subscription history,
  AI credit state, AI purchase orders, and a revenue split showing platform vs AI vs combined totals.

- Added **manual subscription grant** — Super Admin can put a tenant on any active plan, with
  optional custom amount and custom duration, for comps, sales agreements and corrections.

- Added **manual subscription cancel** for the active platform subscription.

- Made both mutations **governed, not silent**: a written reason is mandatory on each (enforced
  in the UI and again on the server), each writes an audit entry recording reason, acting
  Super Admin, plan, amount, duration and the superseded subscription id, and each notifies
  the tenant's admins in-app so the change never happens behind their back.

- Built the **combined revenue analytics** view: combined revenue, current platform MRR,
  platform vs AI split, monthly revenue trend chart, active plan mix, and top 10 tenants by
  revenue — all respecting the selected date range.

- Wired both new screens into Super Admin navigation and routing.

## Decisions worth remembering

- **No database migration was needed.** Subscription rows are already created fresh on every
  successful payment rather than updated in place, so they already function as a revenue ledger.
  A manually-granted subscription is told apart from a real payment purely by having no Razorpay
  payment id — no new column, no migration.

- **Revenue only ever counts real money collected.** Manual grants and manual AI credit
  adjustments are excluded *automatically* as a consequence of that filter, not by a special
  case that someone could later forget to maintain.

- **A manual grant supersedes rather than queues.** The paid flow stacks active + scheduled
  periods back-to-back through a private state machine; replicating that queueing here would add
  real complexity for what is fundamentally an admin correction. A grant cancels the existing
  active subscription and starts a fresh one immediately.

- **A granted subscription behaves identically to a paid one from the tenant's side** — it applies
  the same user status change the real payment flow applies, and cancel only revokes access when
  no other coverage remains, matching the existing cancel path.

- **AI credit management was deliberately not duplicated.** It stays owned by the existing AI
  provider module; this work only reads AI state to render the combined per-tenant picture.

- **MRR is normalized to a 30-day month** so weekly, quarterly and annual plans contribute
  comparably. Platform-only, since AI credit packs are one-time purchases and don't map onto
  "recurring".
