# Connecting Your WhatsApp Business Account

> Audience: a tenant/client administrator connecting their own WhatsApp Business
> account to the CRM. For the as-built engineering reference see
> [WHATSAPP_IMPLEMENTATION.md](WHATSAPP_IMPLEMENTATION.md); for the manual-connect
> internals see [`backend/lib/whatsappTenantConnect.js`](../backend/lib/whatsappTenantConnect.js).

Every organization connects **its own** WhatsApp Business account. There is no
shared platform number: your credentials are used only for your account, your
messages, and your webhooks. Nothing is activated until Meta confirms the
credentials you enter.

**Where:** Settings → **WhatsApp / Meta Configuration** (admin only).

---

## 1. The five values, and who owns each

| # | Value | Where it comes from | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Phone Number ID** | Meta — you copy it | ✅ |
| 2 | **WhatsApp Business Account ID** (WABA ID) | Meta — you copy it | ✅ |
| 3 | **Access token** (System User token) | Meta — you generate it | ✅ |
| 4 | **Meta Business ID** | Meta — you copy it | Optional (reference only) |
| 5 | **Webhook verify token** | **You invent it** — same value in both places | Recommended |
| 6 | **Callback URL** | **We generate it** — you copy it into Meta | ✅ (read-only) |

Three provenance groups, matching the three sections of the Settings form:

- **From your Meta account** — 1, 2, 3, 4. You fetch these from Meta.
- **You choose** — 5. Any random string; it must be *identical* in the CRM and in Meta.
- **Generated by us** — 6. Copy it from the CRM into Meta.

> The **WhatsApp Business phone number** itself (e.g. `+91 98765 43210`) is not
> something you type. Meta returns it during validation and the CRM displays it
> back to you as confirmation that you connected the number you intended.

---

## 2. Prerequisites in Meta

1. A **Meta Business Portfolio** — [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com)
2. A **WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)** with a phone number added and verified.
   The number must not be active on the consumer WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Business
   app — Meta allows a number on only one at a time.
3. A **Meta app** of type *Business* with the **WhatsApp** product added —
   [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com)

---

## 3. Step-by-step

### Step 1 — Phone Number ID and WABA ID

1. Go to [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com) → your app.
2. Left sidebar → **WhatsApp** → **API Setup**.
3. Copy the two ids shown there:
   - **Phone number ID** → paste into *Phone Number ID*
   - **WhatsApp Business Account ID** → paste into *WhatsApp Business Account ID*

> Both are long numeric strings. The Phone Number ID is **not** the phone
> number — if you pasted something starting with `+`, it's the wrong value.

### Step 2 — Access token (use a permanent System User token)

The temporary token on the API Setup screen expires in **24 hours** and will
disconnect the integration overnight. Generate a permanent one instead:

1. [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) → **Business Settings**.
2. **Users** → **System Users** → **Add** (or pick an existing one).
   Give it the **Admin** role.
3. **Add Assets** → **Apps** → select your Meta app → enable **Manage app**.
4. **Add Assets** → **WhatsApp Accounts** → select your WABA → enable **Manage**.
5. Click **Generate new token** → select your app → tick both permissions:
   - `whatsapp_business_messaging`
   - `whatsapp_business_management`
6. Copy the token and paste it into *Access token*.

> Copy it immediately — Meta shows a System User token exactly once. If you lose
> it, generate a new one; the old one keeps working until you revoke it.

### Step 3 — Meta Business ID (optional)

**Business Settings** → **Business Info** → *Business ID*. Stored for reference
only; the integration works without it.

### Step 4 — Webhook verify token

Invent any random string (e.g. `crm-wa-7f3a91c2`). Type it into *Webhook verify
token* in the CRM. You will paste the **same** string into Meta in the next step.
Meta uses it once, to confirm the callback URL really belongs to you.

### Step 5 — Validate & Connect

Click **Validate & Connect**. The CRM calls Meta and checks:

1. the access token is valid and can read your Phone Number ID,
2. that phone number really belongs to the WABA ID you entered,
3. the token's expiry and permissions,
4. then subscribes your WABA to our webhook.

Only if all of that succeeds is the integration saved and marked **Connected**.
If a value is wrong you get the specific reason and the offending field is
highlighted — nothing is activated and nothing partially saved.

### Step 6 — Point Meta's webhook at the CRM

1. Copy the **Callback URL** shown in the *Generated by us* section.
2. In your Meta app → **WhatsApp** → **Configuration** → **Webhook** → **Edit**.
3. Paste the Callback URL, and the **same verify token** from Step 4.
4. Click **Verify and save**.
5. Under **Webhook fields**, subscribe to: `messages`,
   `message_template_status_update`, `message_template_quality_update`,
   `phone_number_quality_update`, `account_update`,
   `business_capability_update`.

Incoming messages now route to your account automatically — the CRM identifies
you from the Phone Number ID on every event, so no two clients can receive each
other's conversations.

## 3b. If the Callback URL changes (operators: read this)

Meta stores the callback URL **on its side**, so it is not something the CRM can
silently update. The URL is derived from the server's `WEBHOOK_BASE_URL`, which
is per-environment:

| Environment | `WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` | Stability |
|---|---|---|
| Local dev | `https://<id>.devtunnels.ms` / ngrok | **Changes every time the tunnel is recreated** |
| Demo | `https://crm.globusdemos.com` | Stable |
| Production | the live domain | Stable |

Consequences:

- Meta requires a publicly reachable **HTTPS** URL with a valid certificate. A
  dev tunnel qualifies, but only while that tunnel is alive.
- Whenever the base URL changes — new tunnel, or promoting dev → demo → live —
  every connected tenant must re-paste the new Callback URL in
  Meta → WhatsApp → Configuration → Webhook. Nothing else changes: their verify
  token, access token, and message history are untouched, and no re-validation
  in the CRM is needed.
- Because a fresh dev tunnel invalidates the registered webhook, treat local
  inbound testing as disposable and do the real webhook registration against a
  stable demo/production domain.
- Set the value as an **origin only** (`https://host`). Pasting the full callback
  URL is tolerated — the trailing `/api/whatsapp/webhook` is stripped before the
  path is appended — but the origin form is the documented contract.

## 4. Errors you might see

| Message / code | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_ACCESS_TOKEN` | Meta rejected the token (expired, revoked, or wrong app) | Generate a new System User token (Step 2) |
| `INVALID_PHONE_NUMBER_ID` | The token is fine but can't read that Phone Number ID | Re-copy the Phone number ID from API Setup |
| `INVALID_BUSINESS_ACCOUNT_ID` | The WABA ID doesn't exist or the token can't read it | Re-copy the WhatsApp Business Account ID; check the System User has *Manage* on that WABA |
| `PHONE_NUMBER_WABA_MISMATCH` | Both ids are valid but belong to different accounts | You mixed values from two accounts — re-copy both from the same API Setup screen |
| `PHONE_NUMBER_CLAIMED` | This number is already connected to another organization on this platform | Disconnect it there first, or contact support. We never move a number between accounts silently |
| `MISSING_FIELDS` | A required value is blank | Fill the highlighted fields |
| Callback URL says *Incomplete* | The server has no public base URL configured | Ask your CRM administrator to set `WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` |
| `WHATSAPP_NOT_VALIDATED` | Something tried to activate WhatsApp without validating first | Use **Validate & Connect** |

## 5. Security

- The access token and verify token are **encrypted at rest** (AES-256-GCM).
- After saving, the CRM only ever shows them masked (`****1234`). There is no
  screen, API response, or log line that returns them in plaintext.
- Credentials are scoped to your organization. Every send, template sync, and
  webhook lookup resolves the credentials of the authenticated organization only.
- **Rotating a token:** paste the new one and click **Validate & Connect** again.
  Leave the field untouched (masked) to keep the current token when editing
  other values.
- **Disconnecting** stops sending immediately but keeps your credentials and
  message history, so reconnecting is just a re-validation.

## 6. Keeping it healthy

The CRM maintains the connection for you:

| Check | Frequency | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Token refresh probe | daily | Extends tokens nearing expiry; disconnects and notifies admins on an expired token |
| Template sync | daily | Pulls approval status and quality rating from Meta |
| Quality / restriction events | live via webhook | Surfaced on the status badge; sending pauses while Meta restricts the account |

Status badge meanings: **Connected** · **Expiring soon** (token expires within 7
days) · **Token expired** · **Webhook failed** · **Quality yellow/red** (Meta
rated your number's quality — reduce blocks and template spam) · **Business
restricted** (Meta restricted the account) · **Disconnected** · **Not connected**.

## 7. Notes for other WhatsApp transports

This page covers the **generic Meta Cloud API** integration used by the standard
CRM WhatsApp surfaces. Two other transports exist and are configured elsewhere:

- **Travel (Wati)** — `/api/travel/whatsapp/*`, its own credentials.
- **WhatsApp Web (QR scan)** — `/api/whatsapp-web/*`, session-based, no Meta app.

Connecting Meta Cloud here does not change either of those.
