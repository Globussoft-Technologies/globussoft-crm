Editorial guidelines

Useful to read. Easy to put into practice.

Give readers something they can try, question, or improve in their own work.

A good fit

What your article needs

  • Send original, unpublished work.
  • Aim for 1,000–1,800 words.
  • Support claims with credible sources.
  • Use clear headings and short paragraphs.
  • Keep it educational, not promotional.
  • Use original or licensed visuals, with attribution.

Not a fit

What we don’t publish

  • Republished, copied, or spun content.
  • Advertisements or affiliate-heavy articles.
  • Topics unrelated to our readers.
  • Plagiarism or thin, AI-generated filler.
  • Spammy or excessive outbound links.

Keep examples responsible: remove confidential customer details and get permission before sharing someone else’s work.
