CORE CAPABILITIES

Everything your sales team needs

From first contact to closed deal — Globus CRM covers the entire customer lifecycle.

AI Automated Calling

AI Agent calls every new lead automatically, no rep ever has to dial. Your team never loses a lead to a missed call again.

AI Automated Follow-ups

AI Agent follows up on every open conversation until a lead responds or goes cold. Follow-ups stop depending on a rep remembering to do them.

AI Lead Scoring

ML engine scores every contact 1–100 based on engagement, company profile, and deal history. Reps know exactly which leads to prioritize first.

Drag-Drop Pipeline

Kanban board with real-time Socket.io sync, all connected users see changes instantly. No refreshing, no stale pipeline views in team meetings.

Agent-wise Reports

Performance leaderboard ranking agents by revenue, deals won, win rate, tasks, calls, and emails. Managers see exactly who's closing, and who needs coaching.

Auto Email Reports

Scheduled daily, weekly, or monthly reports delivered via email as PDF or CSV. Your manager never has to ask for a report again.
